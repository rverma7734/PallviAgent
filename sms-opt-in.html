SMS disclosures

By texting START and then replying YES, you agree to receive conversational SMS messages from PallviAgent about your immigration intake or emergency callback request. Messages may include consent prompts, intake questions, callback updates, and appointment coordination.

  • Message frequency varies during an active intake.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.

Important safety notice

This SMS line is for collecting information for staff review and callback. It is not monitored continuously. If someone is in immediate physical danger, call 911. Do not send documents, A-numbers, Social Security numbers, passport numbers, or other highly sensitive identifiers by SMS.
