SMS Terms and Conditions

Last updated: May 6, 2026

PallviAgent uses SMS for immigration intake coordination, emergency callback routing, appointment coordination, and related client-service communications.

Program Description

When you text the intake number or opt in through a form, the system may send SMS messages related to your immigration intake or emergency callback request. Messages may include consent prompts, intake questions, callback status updates, appointment coordination, HELP responses, and STOP confirmations.

No Legal Advice By SMS

Automated SMS messages do not provide legal advice and do not create an attorney-client relationship. Staff review is required before any legal advice or representation decision is made.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your request and conversation. You may receive multiple messages during an active intake or callback coordination process.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan.

Opt Out

You may reply STOP at any time to opt out of SMS messages. After you reply STOP, the system may send one confirmation message and then stop sending SMS messages to that number unless you opt in again.

Help

You may reply HELP for assistance.

Emergency Notice

SMS is not monitored continuously and should not be used for immediate physical danger. If someone is in immediate physical danger, call 911.

Privacy

For information about how information is collected and used, please review the Privacy Policy.

Supported Carriers

SMS delivery is subject to mobile carrier availability and is not guaranteed. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
