# A2P / Twilio Registration Notes

## Suggested campaign description

PallviAgent is an SMS intake assistant for an immigration law office prototype. Users text the number to provide intake information for attorney review and may receive follow-up messages related to their inquiry.

## Suggested opt-in description

Users opt in by texting the business number first to begin the intake conversation.

## Required keywords

- STOP -> opt out
- HELP -> get help
