Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

PallviAgent respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you contact the emergency immigration intake line by SMS or related intake channels.

Information Collected

The intake line may collect information you choose to provide, including your name, phone number, callback phone number, preferred language, location, basic immigration emergency details, consent status, and message timestamps. The automated intake does not ask for A-numbers, Social Security numbers, passport numbers, or documents.

SMS Information

If you contact the intake line by SMS, the system may collect your phone number, message contents, timestamps, language preference, and basic intake information you provide. This information is used to respond to your request, route it to appropriate staff, maintain records, and support operational obligations.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Personal information collected through SMS is not sold or rented.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to review intake requests, coordinate staff callbacks, respond to inquiries, maintain intake records, improve operations, and comply with applicable legal, ethical, and regulatory obligations.

How Information Is Shared

Information may be shared with staff, contractors, service providers, or professional advisors who help operate communications and intake systems. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, court order, or applicable professional responsibility obligations.

SMS Consent

Users opt in by reviewing the public SMS opt-in page, texting START to +1 (516) 871-4383, and replying YES to the consent prompt. Messages relate only to the user's intake, callback, appointment, or service request. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are used to protect information. No communication method is completely secure. Do not send documents, A-numbers, Social Security numbers, passport numbers, or other highly sensitive identifiers by SMS.

Retention

Incomplete intake records expire seven days after the last message. Completed intake records are retained for up to 30 days for staff callback and operational follow-up. A minimal consent or opt-out audit record may be retained for up to 90 days. Replying STOP removes the intake answers from the active SMS record.

Contact

For SMS assistance, reply HELP to +1 (516) 871-4383.
